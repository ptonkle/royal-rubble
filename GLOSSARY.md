# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this tool means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills, so the glossary cannot disagree with the rules. Keep entries in alphabetical order. `crates/core/tests/copy.rs` checks the format, the placeholders and the `Not:` words; `TONE.md` governs the prose.

### Air effect

A trick you trigger on a shot in flight, such as dropping it straight down. One per shot.

### Ammo

The shots you take into a siege: normal shots and any special ones. Ammo you don't throw is wasted.

### Armory ruin

A spot on the map. A building standing on it gives you an extra shot each round it survives.

### Forecast

Next round's wind, seen early by whoever holds the watchtower hill.

### Ghost

A copy of another player's castle, attacked when the player count is odd. Royals killed in a ghost cost its owner no health.

Not: clone, mirror

### Gold vein

A spot on the map. A building standing on it pays you gold each round it survives.

### Holy well

A spot on the map. A building standing on it heals you a little each round it survives.

### Interest

Extra gold each round for gold you have banked.

### Old ruins

A spot on the map with stone walls already standing. Everyone starts with them free; they sell for nothing.

### Royal

A noble you place in your castle. Each royal killed in a siege costs you health.

Not: VIP, unit

### Segment

One beam of a castle between two points on the build grid: wood, stone, metal or rope. Each material has a longest length.

Not: piece, block

### Undo

Takes back the last thing you placed this build, for all it cost. Key: Z.

Not: revert, rollback

### Spot

A place on the map worth defending, such as a deposit, forest or holy well. A building counts as on it when it stands inside both the spot and your plot.

Not: tile, node

### Stockpile

Wood, stone and metal you have banked. Building spends it before gold.

Not: inventory, storage

### Strain

How close a segment is to breaking under the castle's weight, shown as a color while you build.

Not: stress

### Upgrade

A card you pick in the upgrade phase. It lasts for the rest of the match.

Not: augment, perk
### Bot

A computer player in an open seat. Bots fill every seat no person takes, at Easy, Normal or Hard.

Not: AI, CPU, computer

### Host

The player who opened the room. The host picks the options and starts the match.

### Invite code

A long code to paste by hand when a room code can't connect. The joiner pastes it and sends back a reply code.

### Lobby

The room before a match: the players, the bots and the match options.

### Room code

The six letters that let friends join a room. Copy link sends the same code as a link.

Not: game code

### Wind

Air that pushes every shot the same way. It changes each round and can be turned off in the lobby.


### Watchtower hill

A spot on the map. While a building on it survives, you see the forecast.

### Chain shot

Special ammo that cuts ropes and wood.

### Decoy

A royal that looks real but costs you no health when killed.

### Exploding shot

Special ammo that blows up when it touches something, wearing down every segment nearby.

### Fire shot

Special ammo that sets wood alight. Fire spreads to touching wood.

### Heavy shot

Special ammo: bigger and heavier than a normal shot.

### Kill gold

Gold you earn for each royal you kill.

### Meteor shot

Special ammo: huge, heavy and burning.

### Normal shot

A plain stone from the trebuchet. You get a few each round from your level.

### Producer

A building that makes wood, stone or metal each round it survives: the Carpenter, Quarry and Mine.

### Spread shot

Special ammo that flies apart into several small stones.
### Build

The phase where you place segments and royals. Press Test to see the strain before a siege.

### Drop

An air effect: the shot stops moving sideways and falls straight down.

### Fire

A shot that sets wood alight. Burning wood weakens and spreads fire to wood it touches.

### Heavy

A bigger, heavier shot that hits harder.

### Metal

The strongest material for segments, with the longest reach.

### Normal

The plain shot every player starts with.

### Phase

An air effect: the shot passes through walls for a moment.

### Reverse

An air effect: the shot turns back the way it came.

### Rope

A cheap segment that holds only when pulled. Shots pass through it.

### Sandbox

A practice field with no rules: build a castle, test it and throw shots at it.

### Siege

The phase where everyone throws shots at the castle they are assigned.

### Split

An air effect: the shot breaks into three that fan out.

### Spread

A shot that breaks into three as it leaves the arm.

### Stone

A heavy segment that holds a lot of weight from above but snaps when bent.

### Test

Shows how hard each segment of your castle is working, from easy to breaking.

### Wood

The cheapest segment. It burns.

### Armory

A building that gives you an extra shot next round if it survives the siege.

### Boost

An air effect: the shot speeds up along its path.

### Carpenter

A building that gives you wood each round it survives.

### Chain

A chain shot, on the ammo bar.

### Dive

An air effect: the shot turns straight down and speeds up.

### Exploding

An exploding shot, on the ammo bar.

### Gold

What you spend on segments, buildings, XP and shots. Also the middle tier of upgrade cards.

### Hover

An air effect: the shot hangs still for a moment.

### Level

How far your castle has grown. A higher level gives a wider plot, more shots and more materials.

### Meteor

A meteor shot, on the ammo bar.

### Mine

A building that gives you metal each round it survives.

### Prismatic

The rarest tier of upgrade cards.

### Quarry

A building that gives you stone each round it survives.

### Ready

Says you are done with this phase. The timer ends early once everyone is ready.

### Reroll

Swaps your three upgrade cards for three new ones. The first is free.

### Results

The order players finished in, last one standing first.

### Round

One upgrade, build and siege. Royal damage grows with each round.

### Scout

Looks at another player's castle during the build phase.

### Silver

The commonest tier of upgrade cards.

### Target

The castle you attack this round.

### XP

Experience toward your next level. You get some each round and can buy more with gold.
